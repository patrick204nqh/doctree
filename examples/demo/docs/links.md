# Images and links

## Images

Files in `assets/` open as pictures: [logo.svg](../assets/logo.svg) (SVG also has a Raw view) and [icon.png](../assets/icon.png).

An image inside a page works too:

<img src="../assets/logo.svg" alt="Doctree mark" width="96">

## Hover cards

Hover a link to a trusted site to see a card (needs a network; the list is in Settings → Trusted sites):

- Repo: [doctree](https://github.com/patrick204nqh/doctree)
- Pull request: [cli/cli#1](https://github.com/cli/cli/pull/1)
- Article: [Markdown on Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Markdown)

## Video card

A video link alone on its own line becomes a thumbnail card (click it to watch on the site):

[Never Gonna Give You Up](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ)

A link inside a sentence, like [this one](https://youtu.be/dQw4w9WgXcQ), stays a plain link with a hover card.
